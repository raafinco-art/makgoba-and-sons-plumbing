About us

About Makgoba & Sons Plumbing

Makgoba & Sons Plumbing is a locally operated plumbing company based in Seshego, serving residential, commercial and industrial clients throughout Limpopo.

We inspect the problem, explain the repair clearly and complete the work with the correct materials. That applies whether it is a dripping tap in a Seshego home or a plant room that keeps a business running.

Our aim is straightforward: keep your water systems working, leave the site clean, and be the plumber you call again next time.

How the service works

Four steps from call-out to hand-over

The same sequence on every job, so you always know what happens next and what you are paying for.

Inspect

We find the actual cause instead of treating the symptom, and check what else the fault has affected.

Explain

You get the repair options, the materials needed and the price in plain language before we start.

Repair

The work is done with correct fittings and proper supports, on covered floors and with tools kept tidy.

Test and hand over

We pressure-test the repair, check for leaks, clean up and show you exactly what was done.
